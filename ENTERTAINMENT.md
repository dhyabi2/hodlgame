# HodlGame — Entertainment Feature Plan

Generated by the invention methodology (Methodology-Tree engine seeds → deep evaluation), model `stealth/ox-alpha`. **97 ideas across 8 pages**, every one a deterministic function of data we already index — honest, zero-custody, monochrome-native by construction.

## Build first (highest delight-per-effort, in order)

1. Your Entry Line (Token detail) — small effort, high engagement. Client-side avg-buy line + live P&L delta from the viewer's own wallet; the single best tension-per-pixel change, no backend, no custody.
2. Nemesis Card (Ranks) — small effort, high engagement. Pure read off the existing rank ledger (row-1 / row+1 + gap); turns an intimidating board into a personal chase and the most reliable repeat-check-in driver.
3. Us vs The Whale (Token detail) — small effort, high engagement. Reuses the holders list to spotlight the #1 holder, their % of supply, and their last-hour delta; an instant honest antagonist for near-zero build.
4. Auto-roast captions on pumps & dumps (Home) — small effort, high engagement. Deterministic templates keyed on real event type + magnitude, exported as monochrome share cards; the cheapest off-platform virality lever, honest by construction.
5. Overtake Card (Ranks) — medium effort, high engagement. Detect rank crossings between snapshots and render a verifiable monochrome brag card; pair it directly with Nemesis Card to close the chase-to-share loop in one release.

## Cross-cutting themes (do once, reuse everywhere)

- Honest share cards as the virality engine — Overtake, Auto-roast, In Debt to the Coin, First Holder, Called It all render stark monochrome images from real data; ship one reusable in-app image-export component (respecting the no-download-link constraint, save via OS).
- Personal overlays computed client-side from the viewer's own wallet — Your Entry Line, Diamond Hands Score, Protect Your Entry, Nemesis Card; each turns shared/neutral data into a personal stake with zero custody and zero backend.
- Deterministic narrative labeling over real on-chain events — Archetype Shelves, Rise/Fall/Comeback episodes, Live Drama Chapters, Rank Roast Titles; templated flavor over verifiable event streams, never fabricated drama.
- Verifiable, un-buyable status instead of payouts — First Holder, First-Seen proofs, Diamond-Time / Diamond Hands, Verifiable Trophies, Scout Score; every 'reward' is a replay-provable badge or streak, never a token/prize (avoids custody + gambling smell).
- Loss-aversion and defend-your-position streaks — Diamond Hands, Protect Your Entry, Throne Decay, Scout Score; visible things you might lose, honest because the number only slips when your real share slips.
- Monochrome motion as liveliness and juice — Ink Feedback, Heartbeat Chart, Ripple Rank Animation, live pulse counters, weighted scroll physics; change signaled through weight/opacity/motion (never color) with a reduced-motion fallback everywhere.
- Trust model turned into a feature — Verify-It-Yourself Replay, Creator Net Realized Profit, Rank Time Machine, verifiable trophies; the in-browser verifier the app already ships becomes the entertainment, uniquely defensible and dead on-brand.
- Appointment/live windows for FOMO without custody — Kill Zone (60s sprint), Pump Windows / seasons; real-volume or real-velocity leaders earn a verifiable badge only, the 'prize pot' seed deliberately dropped.
- Rivalry and belonging as retention — Nemesis Card, Us vs The Whale, Faction Standings; a visible opponent or tribe converts solo holding into competitive/team check-ins.
